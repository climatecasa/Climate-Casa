Climate Casa

House of Climate Positive Projects

Climate Casa began with a simple purpose: to educate people about climate and sustainability. What started as a platform for sharing climate knowledge has evolved into a space for creativity, curiosity, and impact. From storytelling and social initiatives to exploring the future of sustainable aviation, Climate Casa reflects the many ways sustainability can inspire, connect, and drive change.

~ Himanshi Shrimali, Founder
